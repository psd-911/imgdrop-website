Frequently Asked Questions

Why do passport and visa forms reject my photos?

Most government, exam, and visa portals enforce strict file size caps (such as maximum 50 KB or 100 KB). Standard image tools usually overshoot or undershoot this limit. ImgDrop automatically calibrates the compression curve in your browser to deliver the sharpest possible image that remains under your exact KB limit.

Are my private photos and ID cards safe?

Yes! Unlike other online compressors that upload your sensitive documents to cloud servers, ImgDrop processes everything locally on your own computer. Your photos never leave your machine.
