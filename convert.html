Frequently Asked Questions — Image Converter

How do I convert WebP images to JPG without losing quality?

ImgDrop decodes WebP images into full RGB pixel data and re-encodes them into clean baseline JPEG at your chosen quality level. Select JPG as the output format, set quality to 92% or higher, and download. The result is visually identical to the original.

Why can I not open WebP images in Photoshop or Windows Photo Viewer?

Many older programs do not natively support Google WebP format. Converting your WebP images to standard JPG or PNG with ImgDrop makes them universally compatible with every photo viewer, editing tool, and operating system.

What is the difference between JPG and PNG?

JPEG (JPG) uses lossy compression that produces small file sizes but discards some detail — ideal for photos. PNG uses lossless compression that preserves every pixel exactly — ideal for screenshots, logos, and images with text or transparency.

Which format should I use for government form uploads?

Most Indian government portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NEET) require JPEG format with specific file size limits (20 KB, 50 KB, or 100 KB). Convert to JPG and use the Target KB feature to compress precisely to the required size.

Are my images safe when converting online?
