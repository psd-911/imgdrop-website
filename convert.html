Why Convert Images with ImgDrop?

How do I convert WebP images to JPG without losing quality?

ImgDrop decodes WebP images into full RGB canvas data and re-encodes them into clean baseline JPEG with custom quality up to 100%, ensuring zero visible compression artifacts.

Why can't Photoshop or older viewers open my WebP images?

Many legacy programs don't natively support Google's WebP container. Converting your downloaded WebP images to standard JPG or PNG with ImgDrop makes them universally compatible with every photo viewer and editing tool.
