Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently, remember user preferences, and provide analytical reporting and relevant advertising.

2. How ImgDrop Uses Cookies & Local Storage

ImgDrop operates as a 100% in-browser, client-side utility. We do not use cookies to track or inspect your uploaded files. All conversions, compression, resizing, and cropping occur strictly within your browser session.

We use cookies and browser local storage for the following specific purposes:

  • Essential & Functional Storage: To store your dark/light theme preference and remember your cookie consent preferences.
  • Advertising & Monetization: We partner with third-party networks, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements that keep our service 100% free and unlimited.
  • Performance & Analytics: To understand aggregate, anonymous traffic patterns and maintain server stability.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the web.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or through AboutAds.info.

4. How Can You Control or Disable Cookies?

You have full control over cookie settings in your browser. You can configure your browser to block, refuse, or alert you when cookies are being sent. Note that disabling cookies will not affect your ability to convert or compress images on ImgDrop, as all image tools run locally in your browser.
