PNG to JPG Conversion FAQs

Why convert PNG to JPG?

PNG files use lossless compression and are often 5x to 10x larger than JPGs. Converting PNG to JPG drastically reduces file size, making images load faster on websites, upload easily to forms, and save disk space.

What happens to transparent backgrounds in PNG?

Because the standard JPEG format does not support transparency, ImgDrop cleanly fills transparent areas with a clean, crisp solid white background.

Is my data safe when converting PNG to JPG?

Yes, 100%! All conversions run on your device's browser memory via HTML5 Canvas. Your photos are never sent to our servers.
