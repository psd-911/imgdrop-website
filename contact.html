Quick Answers

Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. ImgDrop processes everything 100% locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
Why is my compressed image slightly under my target size?
JPEG compression works on blocks of pixels, not individual bytes. The output will always be at or below your target - never above it.
Which formats does ImgDrop support?
Input: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP. Output: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, PDF.
Is ImgDrop free forever?
Yes. ImgDrop is and will remain completely free with no usage limits, subscriptions, or watermarks.
