Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing Guarantee

ImgDrop is designed from the ground up to operate entirely in your web browser. When you select, drag, or process image and PDF files, all decoding, conversion, compression, and editing calculations occur strictly within your local device's memory using standard browser APIs (HTML5 Canvas). Your image data is never uploaded, transferred, or stored on external servers.

2. Information We Collect

Because we do not require account registration or server-side file uploads, we do not collect personal identifiers, email addresses, or your uploaded files. Standard anonymized web analytics (such as page views and browser types) may be collected to help us optimize site performance.

3. Cookies & Advertising

We may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense) to keep this tool free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our official developer publisher page on the Chrome Web Store (Vinland Studio).
