Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing Guarantee

ImgDrop is designed from the ground up to operate entirely in your web browser. When you select, drag, or process image and PDF files, all decoding, conversion, compression, and editing calculations occur strictly within your local device's memory using standard browser APIs (HTML5 Canvas & Web Workers). Your image data and documents are never uploaded, transferred, or stored on external servers or cloud services.

2. Information We Collect

Because we do not require account registration or server-side file processing, we do not collect personal identifiers, passwords, billing information, or your uploaded files. Like most web services, standard non-identifiable web logs (such as IP addresses, browser user-agents, referral sources, and access timestamps) may be generated by our hosting infrastructure to maintain site security and prevent malicious traffic.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • For complete details on our cookie usage and how to control cookies, please consult our dedicated Cookie Policy.

4. Local Storage

ImgDrop utilizes browser localStorage solely for technical and user preference reasons: saving your preferred interface theme (dark mode or light mode) and storing your cookie consent choices. No personally identifiable data is stored.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users are entitled to transparency regarding data collection. Because ImgDrop does not store personal files or sell personal information, your files remain completely confidential and under your direct control at all times.
