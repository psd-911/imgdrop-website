JPG to PNG Conversion FAQs

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is a lossless format, which prevents generational quality loss if you plan to edit the image repeatedly in tools like Figma or Photoshop.

Does converting JPG to PNG make the background transparent?

Converting a JPG to PNG saves the image in the PNG container, but does not automatically remove backgrounds. For transparent backgrounds, use an image editor or crop tool.
