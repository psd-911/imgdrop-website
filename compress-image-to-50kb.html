Standard Photo Specifications for Indian Portals

When applying for recruitment or competitive exams, uploading an incorrect file size is the #1 reason candidates face application rejection:

  • Passport Size Photo: Maximum 50 KB (usually between 20 KB and 50 KB), in JPEG/JPG format.
  • Signature Upload: Maximum 20 KB (usually between 10 KB and 20 KB).
  • Dimensions: Standard 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (approximately 200 × 230 to 413 × 531 pixels).

ImgDrop's intelligent compression algorithm iteratively searches for the maximum possible image quality that yields an output file strictly under 50.0 KB, ensuring sharp contrast and 100% portal acceptance.

Most major Indian government entrance exams and recruitment portals enforce a 20KB to 50KB photo limit, including UPSC CSE, SSC CGL/CHSL, NEET UG, JEE Main, IBPS PO, RRB Railway, State PSCs, and Indian Passport Seva.
